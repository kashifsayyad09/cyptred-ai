# Sample Exam Policy — AI Exam Guardian

## Examination Rules

1. This is a closed-resource examination.
2. Students may not access the internet during the examination.
3. The use of AI assistants (including but not limited to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, ParakeetAI, Perplexity, or any similar tool) is strictly prohibited.
4. Students must remain on the examination page at all times.
5. Copying or pasting text from external sources is prohibited.
6. Opening additional browser tabs or windows during the examination is prohibited.
7. The examination must be conducted in a full-screen browser window where technically possible.

## AI Assistance Policy

The use of any AI writing assistant, code assistant, or AI-powered tool is strictly prohibited during this examination. This includes:

- Web-based AI assistants accessed via browser
- Desktop AI assistant applications
- Browser extensions that provide AI assistance
- Mobile AI assistant applications

Detection of AI-assistant signals in combination with other behavioral indicators will result in the session being flagged for teacher review.

## Review Process

Sessions flagged for review will be examined by the responsible teacher or institution. The teacher will review:

1. The behavioral evidence timeline
2. Risk scores and contributing events
3. AI assistant detection signals
4. Policy context

The teacher makes the final determination. No automated system makes accusation or final verdicts.

## Student Rights

Students have the right to:
- Review the evidence collected about their session
- Provide context or explanation to the reviewing teacher
- Appeal any determination through the institution's standard appeal process
